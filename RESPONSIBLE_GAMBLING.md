# Responsible Gambling and Product Scope

This build is a non-wagering engineering preview. It has no real-money stake, payment, cashout or production wallet functionality. The browser controls are disabled. Do not use this preview to solicit or accept wagers.

Any future real-money release requires separate legal and compliance review for each intended jurisdiction, age/identity controls, self-exclusion, deposit/loss/time limits, reality checks, support/escalation routes, advertising rules and approved game-math disclosures. This document is not a substitute for legal or regulatory advice.
